### 🚀 Google: Accelerate Your Job Search with AI
This course focuses on leveraging Generative AI to streamline the job search process, optimize professional documents, and enhance personal branding. It demonstrates the practical application of AI in managing a career path in the modern tech landscape.

---

### 🎓 Key Learning Outcomes
* **Resume & Cover Letter Optimization:** Using LLMs to tailor professional documents to specific job descriptions.
* **LinkedIn Networking:** Leveraging AI for personalized outreach and profile enhancement.
* **Interview Preparation:** Using AI-driven simulations to practice and refine technical and soft skills.
* **Efficiency at Scale:** Managing the job application pipeline using AI tools to save time and increase impact.
