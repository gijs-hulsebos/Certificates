### 🏫 University of Pennsylvania (Wharton): AI Fundamentals for Non-Data Scientists

Introduces big data, machine learning, model evaluation, emerging methods, and generative AI for business learners without a data-science background.

Official course information: [Coursera course page](https://www.coursera.org/learn/wharton-ai-fundamentals-non-data-scientists).

---

### 🎯 Course Overview

This directory documents the verified course credential and the official module structure published by Coursera. Module notes are concise, factual portfolio summaries rather than reproductions of protected course materials.

| Module | Topics |
