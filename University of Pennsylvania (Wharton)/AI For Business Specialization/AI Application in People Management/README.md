### 🏫 University of Pennsylvania (Wharton): AI Applications in People Management

Examines how artificial intelligence can support human-resources decisions while addressing implementation challenges and emerging approaches.

Official course information: [Coursera course page](https://www.coursera.org/learn/wharton-ai-applications-people-management).

| Module | Topics |
| :--- | :--- |
| [Module 1: The Promise and Potential of AI in HR](./Module%201%20-%20The%20Promise%20and%20Potential%20of%20AI%20in%20HR.md) | AI capabilities in HR; the promise of data-driven people decisions; opportunities and limitations |
